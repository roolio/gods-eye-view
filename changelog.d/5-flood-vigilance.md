- Add the Flood Vigilance (France) layer. It draws the Vigicrues river sections
  in the official green, yellow, orange and red, with green kept faint so a
  flood stands out, and a card per section with its level and bulletin time.
  Below 150 km it adds the Hub'Eau gauging stations of the view with their
  latest water height and hourly trend; a station card shows height, flow,
  measurement time and qualification, and stations silent for six hours are
  grey. The data is fetched keyless under Licence Ouverte 2.0 through a bounded
  same-origin proxy (roolio, roolio/gods-eye-view#4).
